Every voxel in the CT volume was classified into bone / tooth / metal / soft tissue / air by a segmentation pipeline (density thresholds, real 3D connected components, and marker-controlled watershed to separate touching structures). The renderer marches a ray through the actual voxel grid for every pixel and colors each sample by its classification and density — the bone gradient is calibrated from bone-only voxels; teeth and metal are flat, deliberately unmistakable colors.
