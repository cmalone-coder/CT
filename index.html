Every voxel in the CT volume was classified into bone / tooth / metal / soft tissue / air by a segmentation pipeline (density thresholds, real 3D connected components, and marker-controlled watershed to separate touching structures) before any mesh was built. Each structure here is its own independent mesh extracted only from its own labeled voxels — not one mesh colored by a density cutoff after the fact. The bone gradient above is calibrated from bone-only voxels; teeth and metal are flat, deliberately unmistakable colors rather than a continuation of that gradient.
