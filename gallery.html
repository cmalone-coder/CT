CT Maxillofacial Without Contrast

Date of exam: 07/21/2026
Clinical indication: Congenital malformation syndrome (Parry-Romberg), predominantly affecting the right face; fistula; pre-op
Technique: Noncontrast axial CT of the paranasal sinuses. Coronal and sagittal reformats were created.

Findings

Generalized hypoplasia of the right facial structures consistent with history of Parry-Romberg syndrome.

ORIF plate in the anterior wall of the right maxillary sinus. Total of 3 rectangular ossific densities within the right maxillary sinus, 1 of which protrudes into the right nasal cavity.

Surgical resection of the right parotid gland with free flap reconstruction.

Large bony defects (presumably surgical) in the lateral, posterior and medial walls of the right maxillary sinus (images 67 and 72 of series 6) and in the inferior-lateral right orbit (image 49 of series 8).

Large bony defect in the right hard palate with continuous communication from the right maxillary sinus through the inferior right nasal cavity and into the oral cavity through the hard palate defect (image 46 of series 10).

Right-sided cleft palate with thin defect through the anterior right maxilla on images 64-65 of series 6 and image 61 of series 14, extending from the inferior meatus of the right nasal cavity to the right upper lip.

Loss of the normal fat planes in the masticator space/infratemporal fossa posterior to the right maxillary sinus (image 72 of series 4). No clear delineation between the masseter muscle and pterygoids. The right parapharyngeal fat pad is also partially lost.

Soft tissue defect in the right upper lip.

Sinus inflammatory disease: defects present in the lateral, posterior and medial walls of the right maxillary sinus. Very small foci of mucosal thickening along the left maxillary sinus wall, in the bilateral ethmoid air cells, and along the anterior wall of the left sphenoid sinus.

Sinus outflow tracts: ostiomeatal units, frontal ethmoidal recesses, and sphenoethmoidal recesses are patent bilaterally.

Anatomic variance: marked leftward nasal septal deviation. No concha bullosa. Paradoxical rotation of the right middle turbinate. No Haller cells.

Nasal cavity: 1 of the rectangular ossific density foreign bodies in the right maxillary sinus protrudes into the inferior meatus of the right nasal cavity (image 46 of series 10).

Presurgical anatomic considerations — cribriform plate: intact, olfactory recesses symmetric at 2mm bilaterally (Keros 1). Lamina papyracea: intact. Sphenoid pneumatization: post-sellar pattern, no sphenoid septal attachment to the carotid canal. Anterior ethmoid notch: no supraorbital pneumatization above the anterior ethmoid notch on either side.

Impression

  1. Generalized hypoplasia of the right facial structures consistent with history of Parry-Romberg syndrome.
  2. Postoperative changes in the right maxilla with ORIF plate along the anterior wall of the maxillary sinus and large defects, presumably surgical, in the maxillary sinus walls and inferior-lateral right orbit. Prior right parotidectomy and free flap reconstruction.
  3. Total of 3 rectangular ossific density foreign bodies in the right maxillary sinus, 1 of which protrudes into the right nasal cavity.
  4. Right-sided cleft palate.
  5. Bony defect in the right inferior lateral nasal cavity and right hard palate with continuous communication from the right maxillary sinus through the inferior right nasal cavity into the oral cavity (image 46 of series 10).
  6. Loss of the normal fat planes throughout the masticator space/infratemporal fossa posterior to the right maxillary sinus, poorly characterized without contrast. No clear delineation between the muscles of mastication and partial loss of the parapharyngeal fat pad.
  7. Chronic opacification of the right maxillary sinus. Otherwise the paranasal sinuses are essentially clear. All sinus drainage pathways are clear.
  8. Marked leftward nasal septal deviation.
